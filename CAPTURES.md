# Screenshot provenance

All images in `assets/` are direct, unedited viewport screenshots of the **public** [Miami This Week](https://miamithisweek.com/) website. They were captured on **28 September 2026, about 15:10–15:18 America/New_York**, with a Playwright-controlled browser, JavaScript enabled, and the site's English setting selected. They are not mockups or generated renditions of the interface. Event illustrations visible inside the site are editorial ImageGen assets and are identified as such on the event pages.

The agenda and map exclude events whose confirmed upcoming window has passed. At capture time, one event with a noon start and no documented exact end was no longer shown in dynamic lists; the map screenshot therefore shows 27 currently displayed mapped events, while the week's stored agenda had 28 event records with verified coordinates and six without pins. Screenshots are dated evidence of the UI, not live status indicators.

The English “New today” view still rendered its small status badges as “Nuevo” at capture time. The screenshot preserves that visible localization gap as it appeared on the live site; the image was not altered to hide it.
